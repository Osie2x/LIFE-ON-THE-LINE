Printable skeleton

Ch. 7 rules: Inventory cost includes costs to get goods ready for sale. Perpetual sales need two entries. FIFO uses oldest costs first for COGS. Weighted average smooths costs. Ending inventory errors reverse next period. Lower of cost and NRV prevents overstatement.

Ch. 8 rules: Capitalize costs needed to acquire and prepare PPE for use. Depreciation allocates cost, not value. Revisions are prospective. Disposal removes cost and accumulated depreciation, then compares proceeds to book value. Intangibles with finite life are amortized; indefinite-life assets are tested for impairment.
